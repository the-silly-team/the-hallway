MOW MY LAWN 🌱

a game about mowing a lawn that refuses to stay mowed. dig-a-hole loop, but it's grass. no steam, no fee, no nonsense.

⬇ download for windows /source /lawn-inspector
first playable build isn't uploaded yet. the releases page is where the zip will land — no launcher, just unzip and run.

the plan

  • mow → collect clippings → sell → upgrade mower → mow more
  • day/night cycle: grass grows faster at night, headlamp upgrade to see
  • achievements for perfect stripes, 3am mowing, sprinkler incidents
  • something weird buried under the lawn
  • then MOW MY LAWN 2, where the lawn grows forever. trust us.

under the hood

  • godot 4.7.2, native desktop, headless-cli friendly
  • SILLYSOFT original binary asset formats — .sltx, .slau, .slcl, .sllv, .slsc, .slmf
  • menu, settings, persistence, procedural textures and audio all carried over
